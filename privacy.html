Inquiry privacy

When you submit a custom glove specification, BONOECO stores the glove selections, delivery market, email address and notes in its Cloudflare-hosted order database so we can respond to your request. Access to submitted specifications is restricted to the authorized administrator. We do not request payment-card or government identification information through this form.

Your information is used to review and respond to the glove inquiry. To request a copy or deletion of a submitted specification, contact info.bonoeco@gmail.com. Cloudflare may process technical request information to deliver and protect this website.

Avoid including sensitive personal information in the notes field. Email providers process messages under their own policies.
