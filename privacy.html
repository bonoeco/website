Inquiry privacy

The inquiry form prepares a message locally in your browser. It does not submit form entries to a website database. If you send the prepared email, BONOECO receives the information you include to respond to your inquiry.

Your email provider and our email provider process email messages under their own policies. Website hosting may process technical request information to deliver and protect the website.

Avoid sending sensitive personal information or payment-card details. For questions about an inquiry you have sent, contact info.bonoeco@gmail.com.
